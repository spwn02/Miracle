# Miracle Code of Conduct

Miracle is an open-source C++26 foundation library. Technical rigor and respectful collaboration are both expected in its community.

This document sets the standards for participation in Miracle's GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, future Discussions, and other spaces explicitly operated on behalf of the project.

## Our commitments

Every participant, including the maintainer, is expected to contribute to a working environment where people can ask questions, report problems, suggest designs, and disagree on technical grounds without being targeted personally.

We welcome contributors regardless of experience, background, identity, disability, or preferred development tools. Reviews should evaluate code, evidence, and reasoning—not a contributor's identity or perceived ability.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss technical decisions with specificity, evidence, and professional restraint. Strong criticism of an implementation is allowed; attacks on its author are not.
- Be willing to explain assumptions, acknowledge uncertainty, and correct mistakes.
- Respect different perspectives, including contributors who are new to C++26, modules, or Miracle's experimental reference toolchain.
- Make reasonable efforts to communicate accessibly and avoid excluding people through jargon without explanation.
- Respect others' time, consent, boundaries, and confidential information.
- Follow the project's [contribution guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [development protocol](docs/DEVELOPMENT_PROTOCOL.md) where applicable.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following behavior is not permitted in Miracle community spaces:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Discriminatory insults, slurs, or degrading comments directed at a person or group.
- Personal abuse, deliberate humiliation, or sustained trolling, including conduct disguised as a code review.
- Publishing another person's private information without their permission.
- Unwanted sexual attention or sexually explicit material unrelated to legitimate technical work.
- Malicious disruption, repeated spam, or attempts to circumvent moderation actions.
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern or participating in good-faith moderation.

This policy does not forbid respectful disagreement, justified negative code reviews, technical criticism, or discussion of difficult engineering trade-offs.

## Reporting concerns

For project-specific Code of Conduct concerns, contact the Miracle maintainer privately at **[spwn02.dev@gmail.com](mailto:spwn02.dev@gmail.com)**.

Use the subject line `Miracle — Code of Conduct Report` to help distinguish conduct reports from ordinary project correspondence.

When reporting a concern, include only the information necessary to understand the incident:

- A brief description of the behavior.
- Relevant GitHub links and approximate dates or times.
- Supporting context or evidence you are comfortable providing.

Do not disclose passwords, credentials, unnecessary personal information, or confidential allegations in public issues, pull requests, or discussions. You are not required to confront or engage further with the person involved.

**Alternative reporting channels:** For objectionable content on GitHub, use the relevant **Report content** action when available. For violations of GitHub's platform rules, follow [GitHub's abuse-reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

If the concern involves the project maintainer, or you are uncomfortable reporting directly to them, GitHub's reporting mechanisms may provide an alternative where applicable. Miracle currently has no independent project-specific moderation or appeals body.

Security vulnerabilities must follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md). The conduct-reporting mailbox is not a substitute for private vulnerability reporting.

## Moderation and enforcement

The repository maintainer is responsible for interpreting and applying this policy in spaces under Miracle's control. Depending on severity and recurrence, actions may include:

1. Clarifying expectations and requesting an edit or change in behavior.
2. Issuing a warning and restricting further disruptive interaction.
3. Hiding or removing inappropriate contributions, or locking a discussion.
4. Temporarily or permanently restricting participation where GitHub provides that control.
5. Escalating threats or platform violations to GitHub Support.

Actions should be proportionate to the behavior and consider participant safety, context, and prior incidents. The maintainer should avoid conflicts of interest where practical, communicate reasons when safe to do so, and not disclose sensitive reports unnecessarily.

A person affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through the designated private conduct-reporting email address. An appeal does not guarantee reversal. GitHub's own platform enforcement is separate from Miracle's moderation.

Reports will be handled with care and discretion. Information will be disclosed only when reasonably necessary for review, safety, or enforcement. Email is not inherently anonymous or end-to-end encrypted. **Absolute confidentiality, anonymity, and a particular response time cannot be guaranteed.**

## Scope and reference

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in project-run spaces and to people expressly representing Miracle in official project interactions. It does not claim authority over unrelated private communication or personal projects.

The principles were informed by the [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/). This is a Miracle-specific policy, not a verbatim adoption of that document. It supplements GitHub's own community policies.
